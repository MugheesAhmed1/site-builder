# Site Builder library

This folder is the shared library. The builder ships with a copy and, when online, reads the latest version straight from this repository on GitHub. Anything added here shows up in every copy of the builder with no redeploy.

- `design-systems.json` holds every design system in the Library tab: colours, fonts, corners, layout and animation choices. To add one, copy an entry, give it a new `id` (for example `DS-241`) and a `preset` name, change what you like, and commit. The builder checks each entry and skips any that are missing `id`, `fonts` or `accent`.
- To regenerate the built-in 500 from the code, bundle and run `scripts/export-library.ts`, for example with esbuild:
  `npx esbuild scripts/export-library.ts --bundle --platform=node --format=esm --outfile=/tmp/export.mjs --alias:@=. && node /tmp/export.mjs`

- `characters.json` holds the characters in the Characters step: 520 drawn ones and the real-life pack below. Each drawn entry is plain settings: `family` (person, blob, robot, cat, bear, bird, ghost, alien, plant, cup, rocket, orb, dog, tooth, heart, camera, laptop, book, house, car), `palette` (brand, mint, peach, lilac, sky, lemon, coral, slate, ink), `accessory` (none, glasses, hat, headphones, scarf, crown, chef, cap, bow), `expression` (smile, grin, wink, wow), `idle` (bob, sway, bounce, float, wiggle) and a `variant` number that changes skin tone and hair. Add one with a new `id` such as `C-521`.
- `widgets.json` holds the 512 widgets: a `kind` (32 of them, such as stat, chat, rating, calendar, appointment, menu, followers, uptime, growth) and a `skin` (16 of them, such as glass, neon, paper, clay, terminal, blueprint). Add one with a new `id` such as `W-513`.
- `scenes.json` holds the 178 live scenes: a `type` (48 of them in five groups: particles such as name in particles, constellation, vortex, flow field, dot globe and magnetic dots; nature such as fireflies, ocean waves, petals, snow, meadow, night sky, rain, a living tree, an aquarium and sky; your photo with tilt, colour spotlight, photo particles, water ripple and slices; and 3D or physics with glossy orbs, word bubbles, liquid metal, glass shapes and hanging charms on real rope physics; and 22 real-life scenes made from photoreal pictures, 12 of them on full background photos) and a `palette` (your colours, forest, ocean, sunset, night, mono; real-life scenes come in one version). Every scene reacts to the pointer and to taps; many can be dragged.

## The real-life pack

Besides the drawn cast there is a pack of photoreal pictures, so characters and scenes are not only cartoons:

- **Real people, real animals and real mascots** (characters `C-521` on, families `real-person`, `real-animal`, `real-mascot`). Each one has a `src` picture and a `fits` list of the categories it suits, such as a chef for food or a dentist for dentists. They idle, lean toward the pointer and sit on a soft shadow like the drawn ones.
- **Real-life live scenes** (group `real`, 22 of them): a real aquarium, autumn leaves, blossom petals, butterflies, snowflakes, floating seeds, coffee beans and gold flakes, plus 14 layered scenes such as under the sea, drifting clouds, moonlit night, flower garden, café table, forest light, snowy forest, ocean breeze, misty peaks, desert dunes, autumn walk, city lights, tropical leaves and floating treats. Most sit on one of 12 full background photos that shift with the pointer, with cut-outs (coral, clouds, birds, balloons, flowers, food) moving on top. They react to the pointer and to taps.
- **Pictures you can reuse:** 150 in all: 24 people, 24 animals, 12 mascots, fish, particle sprites, props and 12 background photos. Each scene's layers are plain settings in `REAL_SCENES` (`template/src/scenes.js`): a background `plate` and `layers` that each `fall`, `rise`, `fly`, `swim`, `drift`, `float` or `sway`, so new variations can be made from the same pictures without new artwork.

The pictures are transparent WebP files in `public/real/` (people, animals, mascots, fish, sprites), listed in `template/src/real.js`. A downloaded site gets a copy of just the pictures it uses, in its own `public/real/`, so nothing is generated or fetched from an AI service while a site runs.

Every picture was generated for this library from an original written prompt with [Pollinations.ai](https://pollinations.ai) (free, no account), then cut out. None shows a real person, a brand or another site's character. The free service adds a small pollinations.ai mark to each picture. It is never masked: on cut-outs it goes away with the backdrop, and the 12 background photos keep it in their bottom corner. A Pollinations account key gives pictures without it. To add one, put a transparent WebP in the right `public/real/` folder and add an entry to `template/src/real.js`; a character entry can also go in `characters.json` with a `src` that points at the file.

The drawings and animations themselves live in the site template (`template/src/Cast.jsx`, `template/src/Scenes.jsx`, `template/src/cast.css`, `template/src/motion.js`), because a downloaded site needs their code as well as their settings. A chosen character and its widgets are saved inside the site's `site-data.json`, so a published site keeps working even if the library changes. All characters are original artwork drawn in code; none are copied from the reference sites.

## The free public art library

Next to the AI pictures there is a library of open-source illustrations, so parrots, whales, sea life, animals and people look clean and consistent:

- **166 pictures** in `public/real/library/`: 133 from [Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji) by Microsoft (MIT licence, the soft 3D look) and 33 from [Noto Emoji](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) by Google (Apache 2.0, a flat cartoon look with side views that swim and walk well). They were rendered from the sets' SVG sources to transparent WebP, cropped, and animals that move were mirrored to face right like the rest of the scene pictures.
- **Characters** `C-581` to `C-746` in five families: `art-animal` (3D animals), `art-sea` (3D sea life), `art-people` (3D people, mixed skin tones), `art-toy` (3D toys and craft) and `flat-animal` (flat cartoon art). Each has a `src` and a `fits` list, like the real-life characters.
- **Scene concepts**: the cartoon concepts now use this art (cartoon whale, happy whale, dolphins, shark, turtle, jellyfish, octopus, crab, parrot, kite, dinosaur, tiger, elephant parade, pony, dragon, eagle, doves, balloons, lanterns, banner helicopter, sailboat and more), plus particle dolphins and dragon and three new silhouettes.
- **Licences travel with the files.** Both licences allow free commercial use, changes and redistribution if the notice is kept, so `public/real/library/LICENSES.txt` holds both licence texts and any download that uses a library picture gets a copy. `public/real/library/CREDITS.json` lists every file with its source emoji, set and licence, and `template/src/library-art.js` is the manifest the code reads.

Other free sources were checked: Wikimedia Commons and Openverse (public-domain photos) are not reachable from the build environment yet; OpenMoji was left out because its CC BY-SA licence would make every site that uses a picture share-alike.
