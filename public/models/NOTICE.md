# Models

`magic-touch.tflite` is MediaPipe's MagicTouch interactive segmentation model, by Google,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
Model card: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-assets/Model%20Card%20MagicTouch.pdf

It runs in the visitor's browser through `@mediapipe/tasks-vision` (also Apache-2.0) to cut the
subject out of an uploaded photo for photo to 3D. The file is unmodified.
